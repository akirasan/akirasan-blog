Al parecer un "*fantástico"* estudio realizado por los laboratorios [Pegasus Lab](http://www.pegasuslab.com/ "http://www.pegasuslab.com/ ") para la revista sueca [PC För alla](http://pcforalla.idg.se/ArticlePages/200601/18/20060118091910_PFA141/20060118091910_PFA141.dbp.asp "PC För alla"), ha demostrado que **el teclado de un PC tiene 31000 bacterias más** por centímetro cuadrado **que el váter**...

> A test carried out by Pegasus Lab on account for Swedish magazine PC För alla showed that a normal PC keyboard was infected by more bacteria than a normal toilet seat. More specific it contained 33000 bacteria per square centimeter, compared to 130 on a ordinary toilet seat. The tests also showed occurrence of up to 3100 fungi per square centimeter.
