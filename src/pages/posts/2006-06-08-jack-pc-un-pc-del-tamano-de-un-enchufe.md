![jackpc](http://www.akirasan.net/uploads/2006/06/jackpc.thumbnail.jpg)Vía [Microsiervos.com](http://www.microsiervos.com/archivo/ordenadores/jackpc-enchufe.html "http://www.microsiervos.com/archivo/ordenadores/jackpc-enchufe.html"), encuentro este interesante **PC del tamaño de un enchufe!!!**,...y no le falta de nada:

> ([Jack PC](http://www.jadeintegration.com/jackpc.php "http://www.jadeintegration.com/jackpc.php")) The units utilise the latest AMD Au 1550 RISC processors running at either 333 or 500MHz. That's the equivalent of a 800MHz or 1.2GHz x86 desktop processor. They have either 32 or 64MB flash memory to store the operating system (Windows CE .NET 4.2/5) and 64 or 128MB RAM. They can be powered either through a plug on the front or, ideally, using Power over Ethernet (PoE). PoE means the unit is powered over existing structured network cabling from a suitable PoE switch or PoE power injector.
